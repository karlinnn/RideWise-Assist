Ltd. · Customer policy document Page 1 Late Return & Damage Penalty Schedule Document RW-POL-04 · Version 2.1 · Effective 1 January 2025 This schedule sets out the charges for late returns, low battery on EVs, and damage. All penalties attract GST at 18%. 1. Late return fee 1.1 Lateness is measured from the scheduled return time to the actual return time. 1.2 Each late hour, or part of an hour, is charged at 1.5 times the vehicle's hourly rate. For example, 20 minutes late counts as 1 hour; 1 hour 10 minutes late counts as 2 hours. 1.3 Customers in some tiers receive a grace period before a late fee applies. Grace periods are listed in Annexure A. If a return is later than the grace period, the grace period does not reduce the lateness: the full lateness is charged as in clause 1.2. 1.4 Late fee examples (vehicle hourly rate ₹100, customer with no grace period): Returned late by Hours charged Late fee 5 minutes 1 ₹150 59 minutes 1 ₹150 1 hour 1 minute 2 ₹300 3 hours 3 ₹450 3 hours 15 minutes 4 ₹600 1.5 Late fee per hour by vehicle model.
